FAQ

Before you write.

Who is NOVA Impact Lab designed for?

The fictional programme is presented for young people aged 18–30 as well as organisations that support youth innovation, community action and learning.

What kinds of ideas fit the programme?

The showcase focuses on green ideas, digital skills and community action, with an emphasis on concepts that can be built, tested and improved.

How can an organisation get involved?

Potential roles include mentoring, hosting activities, sharing expertise, providing local context or helping participants test early ideas.

Are the impact figures on this website real?

No. The figures are explicitly labelled as demonstration targets for this fictional showcase and should not be read as verified achievements.
