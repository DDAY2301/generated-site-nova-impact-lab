Partnership proposition

Contribute expertise, access or a place to test.

Not every partner needs to do the same thing.

A useful collaboration can provide mentoring, local knowledge, technical expertise, access to young people, venues, networks or feedback on early prototypes. NOVA is built to make those contributions visible and practical.

Where partners fit

Four ways to strengthen the programme.

01

Mentor

Help participants sharpen ideas, make decisions and learn from experience.

Expertise → direction
02

Provide a local context, venue or community where ideas can be explored.

Space → participation
03

Test

Offer constructive feedback and access to real users or stakeholders.

Feedback → evidence

Why collaborate

Partnership should create value on both sides.

Partners gain access to emerging ideas and motivated young people; participants gain context, feedback and stronger pathways to action.

The strongest collaborations are specific: a clear contribution, a clear moment in the programme and a clear benefit for participants and the wider community.
