Why NOVA

A lab for ideas that need somewhere to become real.

Young people rarely lack ideas. What is often missing is the space, structure and support to test them.

NOVA connects learning with practical experimentation. Participants develop skills, work with mentors, collaborate across borders and shape concepts into projects that can create value in their communities.
