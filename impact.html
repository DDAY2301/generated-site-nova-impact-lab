What counts

Outputs are useful. Outcomes matter more.

A prototype is not the end point. It is evidence that an idea has moved from theory into contact with the real world.

The impact framework looks at participation, skills, experimentation and collaboration together. It asks what was created, what was learned, who was involved and what could continue after the programme.

Community value

Impact becomes stronger when people can see themselves in it.

NOVA prioritises projects that can be explained clearly, tested openly and improved through feedback.

That makes results easier to share, discuss and adapt. The goal is not to claim scale too early, but to build credible evidence for what deserves to grow.
